Team Challenge

Test your Masonic knowledge with a growing collection of interactive quizzes covering Freemasonry, its history, symbols, traditions, degrees, and Biblical references.

The collection includes general Masonic knowledge tests along with dedicated Entered Apprentice, Fellow Craft, Master Mason, and Masonic Bible tests. Questions are drawn from larger question banks, so the tests can be taken repeatedly for study, review, or simply for the challenge.

Whether you are a new Mason learning more about the Craft or an experienced Brother refreshing your knowledge, these tests offer an easy and enjoyable way to explore Masonic education.

Because Masonic ritual, customs, and terminology may vary between jurisdictions, these tests are intended for general education and should not replace the official instruction of your Grand Lodge.

Included Resources

  • 1,905 test questions organized into four main categories, covering a wide range of Masonic subjects and levels of difficulty.
  • Supporting eBooks and reference materials are included with all but one test section, providing additional material for study and review.
