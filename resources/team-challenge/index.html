Team Challenge

Team Challenge turns Masonic study into a friendly competition for two to four players or teams. Choose questions from the Entered Apprentice, Fellow Craft, Master Mason, or all three degrees, then take turns testing your knowledge of the symbolism and lessons of Freemasonry.

The game is designed for lodge education nights, degree study, small groups, or simply a little friendly competition among Brothers. Select the degree appropriate for those attending, choose the number of players or teams, and decide how many questions each team will answer.

Questions are randomly selected from the Symbolism of the Three Degrees question banks, and the answer choices are shuffled each time. Correct answers earn one point, scores remain visible throughout the game, and final standings are shown when the challenge is complete.
