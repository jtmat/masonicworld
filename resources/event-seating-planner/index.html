Event Seating Planner

The Event Seating Planner is a simple, free tool for planning seating arrangements for lodge dinners, holiday parties, family nights, banquets, and other events. Create round, square, or rectangular tables, arrange them to match your room, add guests, assign seats, and record meal selections or short notes. Tables and room objects can be moved, resized, and rotated to create the layout you need.

Included Resources

  • Round, square, and rectangular tables with adjustable seating
  • Movable, resizable, and rotatable tables
  • Guest names with optional meal selections and notes
  • Multiple seating plans or sessions within a single event
  • Room objects including a head table, podium/lectern, stage, entrance, and dance floor
  • Printableseating chart and meal sheet
  • CSV meal list for use with Excel or Google Sheets
  • Save and reopen event files for continued planning

Files

Open the Event Seating Planner directly in your browser. No account, installation, or login is required. Event information can be saved to your computer and reopened later.

Tip: If your lodge regularly uses the planner, create a master event file containing your usual members. Save it as a template, then open and save a new copy whenever you begin planning another event.
