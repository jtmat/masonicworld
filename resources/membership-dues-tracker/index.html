Membership & Dues Tracker

Membership and Dues Tracker is a simple lodge record-keeping tool designed for secretaries and other officers who need an easy way to manage member information and annual dues.

Built for small and medium-sized lodges, this tracker focuses on the essentials: a membership register, dues and payment tracking, committee assignments, office history, and mail-merge support for dues notices.

Included Resources

  • Excel edition of the Membership and Dues Tracker
  • Google Sheets edition of the Membership and Dues Tracker
  • Instructions sheet included within each version
  • Membership register for member records and contact information
  • Billing and dues tracker for annual dues, payments, and balances
  • Committee assignments tracker
  • Office history tracker
  • Mail Merge data sheet for notices and correspondence
